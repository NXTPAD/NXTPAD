From wallet to token in three steps

No account to create. Your wallet is your login.

Step 1

Connect and sign in

Choose a network, connect your wallet, and sign a short message. It is free and moves no funds.

Step 2

Set your rules

Name the token, set the supply, then choose your anti-sniping, creator allocation, and early buy-in.

Step 3

Launch for $1

Pay the flat $1 fee, in the network's own coin, and your token is created.

One flat fee: $1 per token

No tiers and no hidden setup costs. The fee is charged once, when the token is created, and paid in the coin of the network you launch on.
